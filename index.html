How it works

Your phone camera picks its own exposure for each photo and records it (shutter, aperture, ISO). From that the meter works out the light level in exposure value (EV). It then checks how bright the metered area came out compared with 18% grey, and corrects for it.

Pick photos straight from the camera. Screenshots, edited or messaged images usually lose the exposure data.

Reciprocity figures come from the film makers' data sheets (Ilford's exponents, Kodak's and Fujifilm's tables). Past the longest time a maker lists, the app extends their curve and says so. Treat that as a starting point and bracket.
